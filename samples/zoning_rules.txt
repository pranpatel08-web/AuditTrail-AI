REGIONAL ZONING AND REGULATORY SOURCE - LUSAKA CENTRAL BUSINESS DISTRICT (SYNTHETIC, FOR DEMONSTRATION)

Section 4B - Ground-floor commercial use.
Ground-floor units in the Central Business District may be used for retail, food and beverage preparation and service, and light wholesale distribution, provided that on-site industrial processes do not exceed 40 percent of the floor area and comply with the District Ventilation and Emissions Schedule.

Section 4B.2 - Food roasting and baking.
Roasting, baking, and similar heat-treatment processes are permitted in ground-floor units only when: (a) the process area does not exceed 30 percent of total floor area; (b) a mechanical ventilation system approved by the municipal fire authority is installed; and (c) a grease interceptor certified by the water utility is installed. Roasting for wholesale distribution is permitted and does not require a separate industrial permit when the 30 percent area condition is met.

Section 7C - Entertainment and licensing.
Premises holding an on-premises liquor license may operate until 24:00 on Fridays and Saturdays. Live entertainment after 22:00 requires a supplementary entertainment permit from the District Licensing Board; the landlord's consent alone is not sufficient.

Section 9A - Signage.
One exterior sign per street frontage is permitted, subject to bylaw approval, with a maximum face area of 3 square meters.

Section 9D - Service bays and deliveries.
Delivery vehicles may use designated service bays between 05:00 and 11:00. Deliveries outside these hours require a written goods-movement plan approved by the District Transport Office.

Section 12 - Assignment of leases.
Commercial leases in the District may restrict assignment; a "sole discretion" landlord consent clause is lawful and does not require amendment.

Section 15 - Parking allocations.
Commercial units must provide a minimum of two (2) parking bays per 100 square meters of floor area.
