COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT - EXCERPT

This Lease is entered into on 1 September 2026 between Northbridge Property Partners LLC ("Landlord") and Verde Coffee Roasters Inc. ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES. Landlord leases to Tenant the ground-floor retail unit at 412 Harbor Street, Suite 100, Lusaka Central Business District (the "Premises"), comprising approximately 220 square meters.

2. TERM. The initial term commences on 1 October 2026 and expires on 30 September 2031 (five years).

3. BASE RENT. Tenant shall pay base rent of USD 3,850 per month, payable in advance on the first day of each month, with an annual escalation of 4 percent.

4. PERMITTED USE. The Premises shall be used solely for the operation of a specialty coffee roastery and retail cafe, including on-site roasting for wholesale distribution. Tenant may not operate a bar, nightclub, or any business involving live entertainment after 22:00 without Landlord's prior written consent.

5. ASSIGNMENT. Tenant may not assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which may be withheld in Landlord's sole discretion.

6. ALTERATIONS. Any structural alteration requires Landlord's written approval. Tenant's roasting equipment requires a dedicated ventilation system and a grease-interceptor approved by the municipal fire authority.

7. SIGNAGE. Tenant may install one exterior sign, subject to municipal signage bylaw approval.

8. PARKING. Tenant is allocated four (4) marked bays in the basement garage. Tenant's delivery vehicles may use the service bay between 05:00 and 11:00 only.
